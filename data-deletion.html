Account and data deletion

You can ask us to delete your Hisaab Page account and the personal information associated with it. You do not need to be signed in to this website to submit a request.

Submit a deletion request

Email us from the Google account used in Hisaab Page. Include the account email address and the words “Account and data deletion request” in the subject. Do not send your password or sign-in verification codes.

Email a deletion request

What deletion covers

  • Firebase account and profile: Your Google sign-in account in Firebase Authentication and the Hisaab Page profile information stored in Firestore, such as your name, email address, and referral ID. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.
  • Records stored on your device: Expense, purchase, contact, budget, and bill-photo records are stored locally in the app and are not deleted by an email request. Delete records in the app, or clear Hisaab Page's storage from your device settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored data on that device.
  • Bill images submitted for scanning: Images selected for bill scanning are sent to a bill-recognition service. We cannot confirm that service's retention period or delete images held by that service through this request. Avoid submitting sensitive images.

After you submit

We will review the request and may ask for information needed to verify it. We will use the email address you provide to communicate about the request. If we cannot verify or complete part of a request, we will let you know. We do not state a fixed completion time here; applicable legal obligations may affect what information can be removed.

Signing out of the app does not delete your Firebase account or profile. A deletion request also does not automatically erase records stored locally on your device.
